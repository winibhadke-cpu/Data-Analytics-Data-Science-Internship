DAY 14 - DAX MEASURES FOR RETAIL SALES DASHBOARD
Source: Day05_Retail_Sales_Dataset.csv | Power BI table: RetailSales
IMPORTANT: The source has Sales, Quantity and Unit_Price but NO Cost, COGS, or Profit column. Therefore Total Profit and Profit Margin intentionally return BLANK().

INSTRUCTIONS: Power BI Desktop > Open Day13 dashboard > Data pane > right-click RetailSales > New measure. Paste ONE formula at a time. Format currency measures as INR; format Revenue % All Categories as Percentage.

01 Total Revenue
Total Revenue = SUM(RetailSales[Sales])
Explanation: Sum of recorded sales. Respects slicers.

02 Total Orders
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(RetailSales[Order_ID])
Explanation: Distinct order IDs, not row count.

03 Total Customers
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(RetailSales[Customer_Name])
Explanation: Distinct names used as a proxy; a customer ID is preferable.

04 Average Order Value
Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Orders])
Explanation: Revenue per distinct order.

05 Total Quantity
Total Quantity = SUM(RetailSales[Quantity])
Explanation: Total items sold.

06 Average Unit Price
Average Unit Price = DIVIDE(SUMX(RetailSales, RetailSales[Quantity] * RetailSales[Unit_Price]), [Total Quantity])
Explanation: Weighted unit price across sold items.

07 Electronics Revenue
Electronics Revenue = CALCULATE([Total Revenue], RetailSales[Category] = "Electronics")
Explanation: Revenue for electronics; preserves other compatible filters.

08 Pune Revenue
Pune Revenue = CALCULATE([Total Revenue], FILTER(RetailSales, RetailSales[City] = "Pune"))
Explanation: Revenue from Pune within current context.

09 Revenue % All Categories
Revenue % All Categories = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], CALCULATE([Total Revenue], ALL(RetailSales[Category])))
Explanation: Category share of revenue with category filter removed, other filters retained.

10 Visible Row Count
Visible Row Count = COUNTROWS(RetailSales)
Explanation: Number of currently visible rows; differs from distinct orders if duplicate IDs exist.

11 Total Profit (unavailable)
Total Profit = BLANK()
Explanation: Required placeholder only: no cost data exists; never show a fabricated value.

12 Profit Margin (unavailable)
Profit Margin = BLANK()
Explanation: Required placeholder only: cannot calculate profit/revenue without costs.

OPTIONAL: AFTER YOU OBTAIN VERIFIED COST DATA (NOT BEFORE)
If a validated row-level Cost column means TOTAL COST for each sales row:
Total Cost = SUM(RetailSales[Cost])
Total Profit = [Total Revenue] - [Total Cost]
Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue])
If Cost instead means PER-UNIT COST, use:
Total Cost = SUMX(RetailSales, RetailSales[Quantity] * RetailSales[Cost])
Do not create these until the correct business meaning of Cost is confirmed.
